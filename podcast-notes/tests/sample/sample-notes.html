The Bread Hour: sample notes

Ep 1 · Marco Bell

Marco Bell started with one small shop and now runs eleven. This page is a made-up test sample. Ads run at 6:00 to 6:45, so you can skip them.
The main idea: Marco keeps things simple. Short recipes, early opening hours and tasting every batch himself helped him grow from one shop to eleven. [11:15]

1. Where he started

  • 1:30At 19, Marco answered an ad for a night helper at a bakery. He did not know what a proofing box was.
  • 2:15Odile, the head baker, lent him a book on French bread. Her rule: "sell out by noon".
  • 3:00He borrowed $40,000 from his aunt and a bank. He bought a used oven for $2,000 instead of a new one for $9,000.
  • 3:45Why open at 5 am: office workers buy bread before 8 am, so the first three hours make about half of the day's money.
  • 4:30The first shop took 8 months to break even. The second took 3 months, because he knew the street by then.

2. The hard year

  • 6:45In 2019 Marco opened 3 shops in one year. Two lost money for 18 months.
  • 7:30He says he signed a lease without reading it. "That was my fault." The rent doubled after year two.
  • 8:15When the flour price doubled, he switched to a smaller mill the same week.
  • 9:00His rule of thumb: if a recipe needs more than six steps, he cuts it. Simple recipes are easier to repeat in 11 shops.

Small things worth noticing

  • 5:15Marco tastes every new batch of dough himself, even with 11 shops. "If I stop tasting, I stop caring."
  • 8:15He called the small mill 14 times before anyone answered.
  • 9:45Dana (host)Dana says she once drove all night to deliver a wedding cake. Marco's reply: "the van is the real shop".

Best lines

"If I stop tasting, I stop caring." — Marco, 5:15
"Hire a manager sooner than you feel ready." — Marco, 10:30

Ideas you can use

  • Cut long recipes. If a task needs more than six steps, remove steps until it is easy to repeat. [9:00]
  • Read the lease. Marco lost money on a lease he did not read. [7:30]
